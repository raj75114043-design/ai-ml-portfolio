# SOP-QA-002 — Management of Out-of-Trend (OOT) Results

> Synthetic SOP for demonstration purposes only. Timelines are illustrative.

## 1. Purpose
This procedure defines how results that are within specification but inconsistent with historical trends are identified, evaluated and escalated.

## 2. Definition
An Out-of-Trend (OOT) result is a result that complies with specification but deviates statistically from the established process or stability trend, indicating a potential shift in process performance.

## 3. Trend Detection Methods
Release data are trended using individuals control charts with limits calculated from at least twenty-five historical batches. Stability data are trended using regression analysis with prediction intervals. Statistical rules include one point beyond three sigma, two of three points beyond two sigma and eight consecutive points on one side of the mean.

## 4. Evaluation
When an OOT signal is detected, QC performs a laboratory assessment within three working days to confirm that the analytical method performed correctly. If the laboratory assessment does not explain the signal, a process evaluation is initiated with Production and Quality Assurance.

## 5. Escalation
A confirmed OOT result that indicates a risk to product quality is escalated as a deviation under SOP-QA-003. OOT trends are summarised in the Annual Product Quality Review and in Continued Process Verification reports.
