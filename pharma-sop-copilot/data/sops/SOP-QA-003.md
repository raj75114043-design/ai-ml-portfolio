# SOP-QA-003 — Deviation Management

> Synthetic SOP for demonstration purposes only. Timelines are illustrative.

## 1. Purpose
This procedure describes how unplanned departures from approved procedures, specifications or GMP requirements are reported, classified, investigated and closed.

## 2. Reporting
Any employee who observes a deviation must report it to the area supervisor immediately and record it in the electronic quality management system within twenty-four hours of detection.

## 3. Classification
Deviations are classified by Quality Assurance as critical, major or minor based on their potential impact on product quality, patient safety and data integrity. Critical deviations are notified to the Head of Quality within one working day.

## 4. Investigation
Major and critical deviations require a documented root cause investigation using structured tools such as the 5 Whys, Ishikawa (fishbone) diagrams or fault tree analysis. Investigations must be completed within thirty calendar days of reporting. The investigation includes an impact assessment on the affected batch and on other batches potentially affected.

## 5. Closure
Deviations are closed by Quality Assurance only after the root cause, product impact assessment and corrective and preventive actions are documented and approved. Recurring deviations are reviewed monthly for trends.
