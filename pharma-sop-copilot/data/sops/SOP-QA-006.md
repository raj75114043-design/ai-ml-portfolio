# SOP-QA-006 — Data Integrity and ALCOA+ Principles

> Synthetic SOP for demonstration purposes only. Timelines are illustrative.

## 1. Purpose
This procedure defines the requirements for ensuring the integrity of GMP data throughout its lifecycle.

## 2. ALCOA+ Principles
GMP data must be Attributable, Legible, Contemporaneous, Original and Accurate, and additionally Complete, Consistent, Enduring and Available.

## 3. Audit Trails
Computerised systems that generate GMP data must have audit trails enabled that record who changed what, when and why. Audit trails for critical data are reviewed before batch release, and system audit trails are reviewed periodically according to risk.

## 4. Electronic Records and Signatures
Electronic records and signatures must comply with 21 CFR Part 11 and EU GMP Annex 11, including unique user identification, secure password controls and signature manifestations showing the signer name, date, time and meaning.

## 5. Access Control
User access is role-based and reviewed at least every six months. Shared accounts are prohibited, and administrator privileges are segregated from users who generate or approve data.
