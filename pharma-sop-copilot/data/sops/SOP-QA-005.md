# SOP-QA-005 — Change Control

> Synthetic SOP for demonstration purposes only. Timelines are illustrative.

## 1. Purpose
This procedure ensures that changes to facilities, equipment, processes, materials, methods and computerised systems are evaluated and approved before implementation.

## 2. Change Classification
Changes are classified as major or minor based on their potential impact on product quality, validated state and regulatory filings.

## 3. Impact Assessment
Each change request includes an impact assessment covering product quality, validation and qualification status, regulatory commitments, stability, documentation and training.

## 4. Regulatory Assessment
Regulatory Affairs determines whether the change requires prior approval, notification or no filing with the relevant health authorities before the change is implemented.

## 5. Implementation and Closure
Changes are implemented only after Quality Assurance approval. The change is closed after verification that all actions, including document updates, training and any required validation, are complete.
