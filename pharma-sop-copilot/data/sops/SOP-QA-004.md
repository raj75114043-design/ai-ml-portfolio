# SOP-QA-004 — Corrective and Preventive Action (CAPA)

> Synthetic SOP for demonstration purposes only. Timelines are illustrative.

## 1. Purpose
This procedure establishes the system for initiating, implementing and verifying corrective and preventive actions.

## 2. Sources of CAPA
CAPA may be initiated from deviations, OOS investigations, customer complaints, audit observations, product quality reviews and trend analysis.

## 3. Action Planning
Each CAPA must address the confirmed root cause, have a named owner and a target completion date. Corrective actions eliminate the cause of an existing problem, while preventive actions eliminate the cause of a potential problem.

## 4. Timelines
CAPA actions should be implemented within ninety calendar days unless a different timeline is justified and approved by Quality Assurance.

## 5. Effectiveness Check
An effectiveness check is performed for every major and critical CAPA, typically three to six months after implementation, using predefined measurable criteria such as absence of recurrence.
